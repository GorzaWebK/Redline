On a phone or tablet: ◀ ▶ on the left steer (or tilt the phone: Settings → Touch steering), GAS and BRAKE on the right, DRIFT is the handbrake. RESET puts the car back on the track, CAM changes the camera and II pauses. Hold BRAKE when stopped to reverse. Settings can also make the car accelerate by itself.

Gamepads work too: left stick steers, right trigger (or A) accelerates, left trigger (or B) brakes, X is the handbrake. Start pauses, Y restarts, Back resets the car, RB changes camera.

Drifting: flick the handbrake into a corner, then stay on the throttle and keep steering into the corner. The car holds the drift while you steer into it. Let go of the steering, countersteer or lift off to straighten up.

The racing line on the road shows where to go: green flat out, yellow turning, red brake.
